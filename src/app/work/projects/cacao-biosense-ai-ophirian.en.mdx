## Overview

**Cacao BioSense AI** is a live AI product developed through **OphirIAn** to translate cocoa sensory perception into a scientific reading of **bioactive compounds**, **aromatic classes**, and **health-effect categories**.

The product combines a full-stack web interface, predictive models trained on sensory data, and **Fleur**, a Spanish-language conversational assistant that explains results to nutritionists, physicians, producers, and food-industry users in accessible language.

## Product Scope

- Designed, built, and launched as an end-to-end AI product with a **Next.js/React** frontend and **Flask/Python** ML backend.
- Containerized with **Docker** and deployed on **AWS** for real-time inference and SaaS-style access.
- Structured around one sensory input surface that feeds three predictive layers: **bioactive compounds**, **aroma families**, and **functional/health effects**.

## Modeling Highlights

- Trained **three Random Forest classifiers** using **2,087 sensory evaluations**, **50 samples**, and **37 sensory attributes**.
- Enriched the prediction space with external evidence from **NIH** and **FooDB** sources.
- Predicted **9 bioactive-compound classes**, **25 aroma classes**, and **30 health-effect classes** from sensory descriptors alone.
- Reached **F1 up to 0.96** and **AUC up to 0.98** in top bioactive classes, with **F1 >= 0.82** and **AUC >= 0.90** across all health-effect groups.

## Interface & Interpretation

- Built **Fleur BioSense**, a Spanish-language conversational AI agent that turns model outputs into domain-appropriate explanations.
- Connected model predictions to an interface designed for scientific communication rather than just experimentation dashboards.
- Framed the product for real users who need interpretable nutritional and sensory guidance without requiring direct chemical analysis.

## Recognition

The project was presented at the **V Simposio de Nutrición: Nutrición con propósito, enfoque pragmático** in Bogotá on **May 29, 2026**.

The exposition earned **second place nationally in the open-category presentations**, strengthening the product’s validation beyond the lab environment.
